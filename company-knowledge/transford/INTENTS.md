# Customer intents on WhatsApp

Transford sells three different things to two kinds of buyer, and WhatsApp is the primary
channel on every page and every product. These are the reasons someone opens that chat.

## HOME_SOLAR
**Customer wants**: to power a home with solar, usually to cut the bill or survive blackouts.
**Sounds like**: "Do you install solar for houses?" · "How much for a 3 bedroom?" ·
"I want backup when power goes" · "Naomba quotation ya solar ya nyumba"
**Needs to know**: goal (savings vs backup), property size, current monthly bill, what must
keep running in an outage, location.
**Knowledge**: SERVICES.md § Solar Installation, PRODUCTS.md § Inverters and Batteries.
**Leads to**: quotation.
**Watch out**: pricing is UNKNOWN. Never size a system or quote a figure in chat.

## COMMERCIAL_SOLAR
**Customer wants**: solar for a business, school, farm or institution.
**Sounds like**: "Solar for our school" · "We run a salon, the bills are too high" ·
"Do you do commercial installations?"
**Needs to know**: type of premises, three-phase or single-phase supply, monthly bill,
location, whether they need continuity of supply during outages.
**Knowledge**: PROFILE.md (schools project), PRODUCTS.md § three-phase inverters.
**Leads to**: quotation, and realistically a call with the team.

## SOLAR_WATER_HEATING
**Customer wants**: hot water from solar.
**Sounds like**: "Do you do solar water heaters?" · "How much for 300 litres?" ·
"Flat plate or tube, which is better?"
**Needs to know**: household size or litres needed, house or flat, existing system.
**Knowledge**: PRODUCTS.md § Solar water heating.
**Watch out**: capacities are not published on the site. Do not confirm a litre size is
available without checking.

## BATTERY_BACKUP
**Customer wants**: storage, or to add batteries to an existing setup.
**Sounds like**: "How much is a Deye battery?" · "I have panels, I need backup" ·
"Is 5kWh enough for my house?"
**Needs to know**: existing inverter and panels, what they want to run and for how long.
**Knowledge**: PRODUCTS.md § Batteries (Deye SE-F5, SE-F5 Pro).
**Watch out**: specs are verified, prices are not.

## EXISTING_SYSTEM_PROBLEM
**Customer wants**: an existing system fixed, upgraded, or serviced.
**Sounds like**: "My inverter is beeping" · "Panels are not charging" ·
"Can you service a system you didn't install?"
**Needs to know**: what the system is, who installed it, what it is doing now, location.
**Knowledge**: SERVICES.md § Maintenance Services.
**Watch out**: whether they service third-party systems is UNKNOWN. Do not promise it —
take the details and hand to a person.

## HVAC
**Customer wants**: air conditioning or ventilation, home or office.
**Sounds like**: "Do you install AC?" · "How much for aircon in a 3 bedroom?" ·
"We need ventilation for a shop"
**Needs to know**: number of rooms or floor area, home or business, location.
**Knowledge**: SERVICES.md § HVAC Solutions, PRODUCTS.md § HVAC.

## SECURITY_SYSTEMS
**Customer wants**: CCTV, alarms, electric fencing or automated gates.
**Sounds like**: "Do you install CCTV?" · "Electric fence for my farm" ·
"How many cameras for a maisonette?" · "Do you do automatic gates?"
**Needs to know**: which of the four (CCTV / alarm / fence / gate), property type and size,
location, and whether remote monitoring is wanted.
**Knowledge**: SERVICES.md § Security Systems, PRODUCTS.md § Security hardware.
**Watch out**: monitoring contracts are UNKNOWN.

## PRICE_QUERY
**Customer wants**: a number, immediately, often as their first message.
**Sounds like**: "Bei?" · "How much?" · "Send me your price list"
**Needs to know**: what for, and enough context to route a quote.
**Leads to**: quotation — never a figure in chat, because none is published.
**Handling**: answer honestly that pricing is per project, then ask the one question that
moves it forward. This is the single most common intent and the one where invention is most
tempting.

## SITE_VISIT_OR_SHOWROOM
**Customer wants**: to see the products, or have someone come and look.
**Sounds like**: "Where are you located?" · "Can I come see?" · "Can someone come assess?"
**Knowledge**: CONTACT.md — Ruiru, bypass, opposite Mark Hotel.
**Watch out**: opening hours are UNKNOWN, and a site visit is not an advertised service.
Confirm both with the team rather than promising.

## COVERAGE
**Customer wants**: to know if Transford works in their area.
**Sounds like**: "Do you come to Nakuru?" · "Are you in Mombasa?" · "Do you install in Uganda?"
**Knowledge**: PROFILE.md — Ruiru base, claimed 47-county coverage, past jobs in Machakos,
Kiambu, Murang'a, Kilifi and Mwanza.
**Watch out**: travel charges and area limits are UNKNOWN.

## HUMAN_SUPPORT
**Customer wants**: a person — for a complaint, an existing job, or anything the assistant
cannot verify.
**Sounds like**: "Let me talk to someone" · "I paid and nobody has come" · "This is urgent"
**Handling**: hand over immediately with the conversation summary. Never argue, never
improvise a resolution.

## NOT_OUR_BUSINESS
Anything unrelated to solar, HVAC, security or the company itself. Redirect once, warmly:
"I'm here to help with Transford's solar, HVAC and security services. What can I help you
with today?" Do not answer the off-topic question, and do not repeat the redirect more than
once per topic — if they persist, hand to a human.
