## What the company asks for up front
The contact form's own fields — the closest thing to an official qualification list:
- Name
- E-mail Address
- Phone No
- Service Name (dropdown: Solar Solution / HVAC Solution / Security Systems)
- Message

Note what it does *not* ask: budget, property type, load size, timeline or location. Anything
beyond the five fields above is our inference about what would help them, not their process.

## Qualification signals that matter to this business
Derived from what they sell, not from a published process:
- Which line — solar, HVAC or security (their own form splits on this first)
- Home or business
- Location, since installation requires travel from Ruiru
- For solar: the goal (cut the bill vs. survive blackouts), property size, current monthly
  electricity spend, and what must keep running during an outage — these determine system and
  battery sizing
- Timeline and whether the customer already has a system
