## How to handle these in customer conversations
| Asked about | Say |
|---|---|
| Price | "We quote per project since it depends on your load and site. Tell me about the property and I'll get the team on it." |
| Financing | "Let me check what payment options we have and come back to you." |
| Warranty | "I'll get you the exact warranty terms rather than quote you something wrong." |
| How long it takes | "That depends on the system — I'll have the team confirm a timeline with your quote." |
| Hours | "Let me confirm our hours. In the meantime, what can I help you with?" |
| Whether they cover an area | "We work across Kenya and have done jobs in Machakos, Murang'a and Kilifi. Where are you? I'll confirm what's involved." |
| Certifications | "I'll get the team to confirm our licensing details for you." |

Never fill any of these with an industry-typical figure. "Most solar installs take 2–3 days"
is true of the industry and worthless as a promise from this company.

## Ask the company these questions first
1. What are your price ranges, or at least starting prices, for a typical home solar system?
2. What warranty do you give on equipment and on workmanship?
3. What are your opening hours, and which number should customers use — 0740554444 only?
4. How long from accepted quote to completed installation?
5. Do you offer any payment plan or financing?
6. Which counties do you actually cover without a travel charge?
7. Is the Ruiru Membly showroom the same as the bypass address, or a second location?
8. What does "Maintenance Services" cover, and do you service systems you didn't install?
9. Are you EPRA-licensed, and can we publish the licence number?
10. Which is right — 700+ projects or 300 clients?
