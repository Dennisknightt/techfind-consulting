# Brand

## Voice
Direct, confident, benefit-first, and comfortable making bold claims. Sentences are short and
declarative. Headline claims are stated as fact ("We are Number one at solving your Energy
bills", "the #1 Solar Company in Kenya & East Africa"). The register is commercial rather than
technical — the customer benefit leads, the engineering detail follows only on product pages.

Quoted examples to imitate:
- "We are Number one at solving your Energy bills by providing affordable Solar, HVAC &
  Security systems."
- "We strive to eliminate electricity bills and power blackouts…"
- "Let's Chat!"
- "Total Visual Authority." (CCTV)
- "Take absolute control of your energy…" (inverters)

The product copy leans on strong, slightly punchy phrases like those last two. Copy written
for this brand can be assertive; it should not be cautious or academic.

## Words they use
| They say | Not |
|---|---|
| Solar Solution / HVAC Solution / Security Systems | "packages", "kits" |
| Request Quote / Get Quote | "buy now", "order" |
| Maintenance Services | "servicing plan", "support contract" |
| Energy bills, power blackouts | "grid outages", "load shedding" |
| Let's Chat! | "Contact us" |

Kenyan context matters: prices are in Kenyan Shillings (KSh), customers are in counties
(Kiambu, Machakos, Murang'a, Kilifi), and the audience understands blackouts and monthly
electricity bills as the core problem.

## How they treat customers
Consultative but quote-driven. Every path leads to a conversation with a person — there is no
self-service purchase, no published price list, and WhatsApp is offered on every page and
every product. The brand's implicit promise is access: message us and we will sort you out.

## Do / don't for anyone writing as this company
**Do**
- Lead with the benefit: lower bills, no blackouts, security.
- Be brief and confident.
- Use KSh, county names, and WhatsApp as the natural next step.
- Point to the TikTok for proof of real installations.

**Don't**
- Quote a price, warranty, lead time or financing term — none are published (see UNKNOWN.md).
- Name any of the six people on /our-team/; that page is template demo content.
- Repeat the "700+ projects" and "300 happy clients" figures in the same breath — the site
  contradicts itself. Prefer "hundreds of installations" or cite neither.
- Use the Google Maps location from the contact page; it points to California.
