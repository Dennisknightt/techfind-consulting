## Products and technical

**What batteries do you use?**
Deye lithium storage — the SE-F5 and SE-F5 Pro, both 5.12 kWh LiFePO4, rated over 6,000
cycles at 80% depth of discharge. [product pages]

**What size inverter do I need?**
Transford stocks Deye hybrid inverters from 3kW single-phase up to 20kW three-phase, in
single, split and three-phase. Which one suits a given property depends on load and phase
supply — that is a sizing question for the team, not something to answer from the catalogue.

**Do you do solar water heaters?**
Yes — tube, flat plate single panel and flat plate double panel models. Capacities are not
published. [shop]

**Do you do CCTV and electric fencing?**
Yes — CCTV, burglar alarms and sensors, electric fencing (home, farm, razor wire) and
automatic gates. [shop, services]
