## Discrepancies

1. **Projects vs clients.** Homepage: "700 +" completed projects. About page: "300 Happy
   Clients". Both are on the official site and are not reconcilable as written. Do not cite
   either as a fact.
2. **Address.** Site says "Ruiru, Bypass - (Opp. Mark Hotel)". TikTok says "showroom at Ruiru
   membly along Eastern Bypass". Possibly the same place, possibly two. Unresolved.
3. **Phone.** Site publishes 0740554444 only; a TikTok caption uses 0708527974. Unresolved.
4. **TikTok handle.** The site links @transford.solutions; search results return
   @transford.solutio. Unresolved — check which account is live before publishing it.
5. **Map location.** The contact page's Google Maps embed resolves to Venice, California — a
   template default the company has not replaced. The Ruiru address is the real one.
