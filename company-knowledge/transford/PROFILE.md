# Transford Solutions Ltd — company profile

## What they do
Transford Solutions Ltd sells and installs solar power, HVAC (air conditioning and
ventilation) and security systems, and services them afterwards. The site's own summary:
"We are Number one at solving your Energy bills by providing affordable Solar, HVAC &
Security systems." It describes itself as a "leading provider of solar energy, HVAC &
Security solutions in Kenya and East Africa". [homepage]

## Industry
Renewable energy installation and electrical contracting, with adjacent HVAC and physical
security lines. Sells both as a contractor (installation projects) and as a retailer (an
online shop of panels, inverters, batteries, lights and security hardware).

## Positioning
Affordability plus engineering competence, aimed at eliminating power bills and blackouts.
Tagline: "Affordable Solar | HVAC | Security Systems | Maintenance Services". [homepage]

Mission: "We strive to eliminate electricity bills and power blackouts, through our
top-quality and reliable products, making solar energy, accessible and beneficial for all."
Vision: "We aim to lead the way in providing sustainable, affordable, and efficient solar
energy solutions that empower individuals and businesses to harness the power of the sun."
[about-us]

## Customers they serve
Both homes and businesses. The catalogue marks HVAC as "Residential and Commercial", the
portfolio includes a residential project and a schools project, and the vision statement
names "individuals and businesses". Testimonials reference customers in Machakos, Kiambu,
Murang'a, Kilifi and Mwanza (Tanzania). [about-us, shop, case-studies]

## Where they operate
Based in Ruiru, Kiambu County, at "Ruiru, Bypass - (Opp. Mark Hotel)". The homepage claims
coverage of "47 counties and East Africa at Large". Whether that means they will travel
anywhere in Kenya, and on what terms, is UNKNOWN / REQUIRES COMPANY CONFIRMATION.
[homepage, contact-us]

## Business model
Two revenue lines visible: installation projects quoted individually (every product and
service routes to "Request Quote" or WhatsApp rather than a checkout price), and maintenance
services. A WooCommerce shop exists with a cart and checkout, but no product carries a real
price, so the shop functions as a catalogue that generates quote requests. [shop, product pages]
